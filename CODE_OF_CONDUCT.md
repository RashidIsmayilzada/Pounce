# Code of conduct

Pounce follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated in issues, pull requests or any other project space.

To report unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/RashidIsmayilzada). Reports are handled privately, and the maintainer may remove comments or block contributors who break these rules.
